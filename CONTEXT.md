# ArtFind

ArtFind is a discovery and matching directory that indexes public visual illustrators and digital artists who are open to commissions, matches them to a Commissioner's needs, and redirects Commissioners to the Artist's external page.

## Language

**Artist**:
A visual illustrator or digital artist whose public profile and work are indexed by ArtFind for commission discovery.
_Avoid_: Creator, user, seller, vendor

**Artist Page**:
The primary external webpage (preferring an Artist's commission or portfolio link such as VGen, Carrd, or Ko-fi, falling back to their Source Profile) that ArtFind redirects Commissioners to so they can view work or initiate a commission.
_Avoid_: Profile, listing URL, destination link

**Source Profile**:
An Artist's native account on a Source Platform (such as a Bluesky account identified by its AT Protocol DID), used for ingestion, Commission Status re-verification, and attribution.
_Avoid_: Social account, handle, upstream user

**Commissioner**:
A person searching ArtFind to discover and hire an Artist for commissioned artwork.
_Avoid_: Client, buyer, user, customer, seeker

**Commission Brief**:
The requirements, preferences, and constraints a Commissioner provides to find matching Artists.
_Avoid_: Query, prompt, request, search

**Commission Status**:
Whether an Artist is currently accepting new commissioned work.
_Avoid_: Availability, open flag, status

**Source Platform**:
A public website or feed (such as Reddit, Bluesky, or VGen) from which ArtFind discovers and indexes Artists.
_Avoid_: Scraper target, provider, channel
